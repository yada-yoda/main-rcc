01 — About

Defined

RIZZO (proper noun)

Pronunciation: ri·zzo [/r IH — z oh/]
  1. A surname of Italian origin, often denoting a familial or ancestral lineage.
  2. Not a Jerky Boy.
  3. Not the Philadelphia mayor.
  4. Not a rat from the Muppets.
  5. Not related to Betty Rizzo, leader of the "Pink Ladies".

I don't understand. Frank, if you can be so charismatic and so likable and get whatever you want… why don't you just go get what you want?

Frank Rizzo

Chicago

Frank draws on diverse life experiences to develop his acting, improvisation, and on-camera skills through the Conservatory program at The Second City and Acting Studio Chicago. A working background in music management and finance brings authentic professional-world texture to character work on screen. Selected credits include Waiter Boys and Deadly Domicile.
